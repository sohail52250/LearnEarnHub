What suppliers can provide

  • Business and supplier profile
  • Available product or service
  • Unit price and total price
  • Delivery cost and estimated date
  • Quotation validity and terms
  • Verified business information

Customers should be able to compare more than price: supplier verification, delivery time, quality/specification fit and terms matter too.
