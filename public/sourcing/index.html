Quotation comparison

Each quotation can include unit price, quantity, delivery cost, estimated delivery time, validity and terms. The system should help the customer compare total cost + delivery + supplier verification + fit + terms, not just the cheapest number.

Complete commercial workflow

Customer need → discovery → supplier contact → quotation → price comparison → supplier selection → quotation acceptance/order → delivery tracking → customer confirmation → payment

This is a private commercial marketplace workflow. It does not introduce government procurement or administrative approval into LearnEarnHub.
