Customer discovery

Start with the customer's real need.

A good sourcing process does not begin with a product. It begins by understanding the problem, desired outcome, constraints and context.

1. Understand

What problem are you trying to solve?

2. Clarify

What result would make the customer satisfied?

3. Define

Capture specifications, quantity, budget and timing.

4. Source

Find suppliers that fit the actual requirement.

Discover and document the need

Keep customer contact details private by default. Only authorized users should access personal contact information.

Why this matters

  • The customer may describe a solution before the real problem is understood.
  • Better discovery reduces unsuitable supplier matches and wasted quotations.
  • Requirements can be compared consistently across suppliers.
  • Customer satisfaction can be measured against the original need.
